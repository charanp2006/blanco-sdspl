### Service 1: Structural Steel Detailing
- **Overview:** Structural steel detailing covers the production of shop drawings, erection drawings, and associated documentation from structural design drawings. At Blanco, this work is produced in Tekla Structures and checked against AISC provisions.
- **Process:** Drawing Review → 3D Modelling → Shop Drawings → Erection Drawings → QC & Checking
- **Deliverables:** Tekla 3D model (.tekla / IFC) · Shop drawings (PDF/DWG) · Erection drawings · Bolt lists & material take-offs
- **Benefits:** AISC-compliant detailing · Dedicated checking department for every drawing set · Experience across 1,000+ completed projects · USA time-zone-aligned turnaround
- **FAQ 1:** Q: Do you provide connection design or only detailing? — A: Both are available — connection design support (via RISA/RAM/IDEA StatiCa) alongside full Tekla-based detailing, depending on the project scope.
- **FAQ 2:** Q: What formats do you deliver models and drawings in? — A: Tekla native files, IFC for interoperability, and PDF/DWG drawing sets.
- **Related:** Connection Design, Tekla Modelling

### Service 2: Connection Design
- **Overview:** Connection design involves sizing steel connections and verifying them against AISC codes. At Blanco, this work is performed alongside detailing so that geometry, capacity, and drawings remain consistent.
- **Process:** Load Review → Connection Sizing → AISC Code Check → Model Integration
- **Deliverables:** Connection design calculations · AISC code-check reports · Integrated Tekla connections
- **Benefits:** Geometry and capacity handled in one workflow · Reduced RFIs during fabrication
- **FAQ:** Q: Which code do you design to? — A: AISC (American Institute of Steel Construction) provisions.
- **Related:** Structural Steel Detailing, Tekla Modelling

### Service 3: Tekla Modelling
- **Overview:** Tekla Structures modelling produces detailed 3D models of structural steel framing. These models can serve as the basis for drawing generation, clash coordination, or as standalone deliverables.
- **Process:** Model Setup → Structural Modelling → Clash Coordination → Deliverable Export
- **Deliverables:** Tekla 3D model · IFC export · Clash/coordination report
- **Benefits:** Model-based accuracy · Foundation for downstream drawing generation · Tekla-certified modelling team
- **FAQ:** Q: Can you work from an existing Tekla model? — A: Yes — existing Tekla models can be picked up, extended, or corrected mid-project.
- **Related:** Structural Steel Detailing, Material Take-offs

### Service 4: Material Take-offs
- **Overview:** Material take-offs are extracted directly from the Tekla model, producing quantities for bolts, plates, and members that correspond to the detailed drawings.
- **Process:** Model Finalization → Quantity Extraction → Report Formatting
- **Deliverables:** Bolt lists · Bill of materials (BOM) · Bill of quantities (BOQ)
- **Benefits:** Model-accurate quantities · Consistent with fabrication drawings
- **FAQ:** Q: Can take-offs be split by phase or package? — A: Yes — quantities can be broken out by erection sequence, package, or shipment.
- **Related:** Tekla Modelling, Structural Steel Detailing

### Service 5: AutoCAD Drafting Support
- **Overview:** Standalone 2D drafting in AutoCAD for shop drawings, layout plans, and related documentation — available as a support service independent of full 3D detailing.
- **Process:** Reference Review → Drafting → Checking
- **Deliverables:** AutoCAD drawing sets (DWG/PDF)
- **Benefits:** Available as a standalone service · Suitable for smaller or drawing-only scopes
- **FAQ:** Q: Is this available independently of Tekla-based detailing? — A: Yes — AutoCAD drafting support can be engaged on its own.
- **Related:** Structural Steel Detailing
